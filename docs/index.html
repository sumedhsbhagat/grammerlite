Private writing help that never leaves your device.

Fix grammar, sharpen clarity, or change the tone of your English text, using Apple Intelligence running on your iPhone or iPad.

Correct

Fixes grammar, spelling, punctuation, and texting shorthand, without rewriting your voice.

Clarify

Tone

Rewrites in a professional, friendly, confident, or neutral tone.

Use it in any app

Select text in Mail, Notes, Messages, or any other app, tap Share, and choose GrammarLite.

Private by design

Your text is processed on your device by Apple's on-device model. It is never sent to a server, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • An iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
  • GrammarLite currently works with English text only.

Pricing

10 free rewrites a day. GrammarLite Pro unlocks unlimited rewrites with a monthly subscription that includes a free trial for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency.

Frequently asked questions

GrammarLite says on-device help is unavailable.

GrammarLite needs an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence. Make sure it's turned on in Settings ▸ Apple Intelligence & Siri. Right after you turn it on, the model may need a few minutes to download; GrammarLite shows "Preparing on-device model…" until it's ready.

How do I use GrammarLite in Mail, Notes, or other apps?

  1. Select the text you want to improve.
  2. Tap Share… in the menu above the selection.
  3. First time only: swipe the row of app icons to the end, tap More, and add GrammarLite to your Favorites.
  4. Tap GrammarLite, choose a mode, tap Improve, then Copy and paste over your text.

You'll also find these steps in the app under Settings ▸ Use in Other Apps.

It says my text doesn't look like English.

GrammarLite currently works with English text only. If your text is English and you see this warning, tap Improve Anyway.

How many free rewrites do I get?

10 rewrites a day, shared between the app and the Share menu. The count resets each day. GrammarLite Pro removes the limit.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open GrammarLite, and tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the upgrade screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In GrammarLite, go to Settings ▸ Manage Subscription, or on your device open Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Does GrammarLite send my text anywhere?

No. Your text is processed on your device and never sent to a server. See the privacy policy.

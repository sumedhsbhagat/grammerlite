Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: GrammarLite processes your text on your device. Your text is never sent to us or to any server. We don't collect personal data, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Your text

When you use GrammarLite, in the app or from the Share menu in another app, your text is processed on your iPhone or iPad by Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). Your text is not transmitted to, stored on, or logged by any server operated by us or by third parties.

History stored on your device

If you choose to keep history, your past rewrites are stored only on your device. You control this in Settings ▸ Save history:

  • Don't save history: nothing is kept after you leave a rewrite.
  • Keep for 30 days: entries are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Keep until deleted: entries stay until you delete them.

You can delete individual entries or clear all history at any time. Deleting the app removes its history. Like other data on your device, history may be included in backups of your device that you make with iCloud or a computer; those backups are managed by you and Apple, not by us.

Settings and usage counts

GrammarLite stores your preferences (such as default mode and tone) and a count of free rewrites used today on your device, so the daily free limit works in both the app and the Share menu. This information never leaves your device.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions to GrammarLite Pro are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The app asks the App Store only whether you have an active subscription. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

GrammarLite contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software. The app writes technical logs only to your device's system log, and those logs never include the text you write. If you have chosen to share analytics with Apple in your device settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect, under Apple's privacy terms.

Children

GrammarLite does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
